# Dewey — Positioning & Friend Pitch

**Live:** https://deweybooks.vercel.app

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Your library card's secret superpower.

**The problem it solves:** Everyone has a want-to-read list and a library card — but checking whether the library *actually has* 15 books, on the shelf, at your branch, means 15 separate catalog searches. So people buy the book or forget it.

**The wedge:** Dewey does the whole list in one shot, tells you what's physically on the shelf at YOUR branch right now, and if you don't have a list, an AI builds one from your mood. No login. No app store. Free books you already pay taxes for.

**Positioning against the obvious question ("isn't this just the library website?"):**
The catalog answers "does the system own it?" Dewey answers "can I walk in Saturday and grab it?" — for your whole list at once, plus what to read next.
