# Book Scout — Branding

## Name Candidates

| # | Name | Rationale | Vibe |
|---|------|-----------|------|
| 1 | **Book Scout** | Working name. Direct, action-oriented. "Scout" signals exploration & discovery. | Friendly, mission-driven. |
| 2 | **Stack Peach** | "Stack" → library shelves. "Peach" → Atlanta. Double meaning on discovery (stacking finds). | Playful, Southern, memorable. |
| 3 | **Shelf Finder** | Literal + optimizable for search. Clear hook: "I found it on Shelf Finder." | Utilitarian, trustworthy. |
| 4 | **Ponce Pages** | Ponce City corridor (Atlanta landmark). Literary nod: "pages." | Local, cultured. |
| 5 | **Fulton's Find** | Fulton County Library. "Find" = mission. Possessive warmth. | Warm, specific. |
| 6 | **Spine Hunt** | "Spine" = book edge on shelf. "Hunt" = treasure, urgency. | Playful, bookish. |

**Collision check:** Stack Peach (clear), Shelf Finder (low risk—exists as phrase but no major SaaS), Ponce Pages (low risk—niche Atlanta ref), Fulton's Find (safe—descriptive), Spine Hunt (safe—whimsical). Book Scout checks as common concept, not a dominant brand. **No hard blocks.**

## Top 3 Names — Logo Prompts

### **Stack Peach**

**Prompt 1 (Minimal Geometric Mark):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Flat vector mark only. A single stylized peach silhouette integrated with 2–3 stacked horizontal lines (books/shelves) inside or behind it. Peach in teal #2DD4BF, lines in purple #8B5CF6. No gradients, no shadows. Proportions: 64px @ 1x. Negative space clean.

**Prompt 2 (Literary/Bookish Illustration):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Cozy bookshelf corner illustration. Single peach sitting on a shelf between two books, teal spine to the left, purple spine to the right. Flat vector, no photorealism. Warm line weights, hand-drawn feel but precise. Peach in teal #2DD4BF, books in purple #8B5CF6 + accent colors. 64px @ 1x, crisp at small scale.

**Prompt 3 (Wordmark-Focused):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Custom wordmark: "Stack Peach" in a clean, modern sans-serif. Integrate a small peach icon above or within the "Stack" letterforms. Text in teal #2DD4BF, peach accent in purple #8B5CF6. Flat, no gradients. 64px logo mark width @ 1x, leaving room for word-mark lockup.

---

### **Shelf Finder**

**Prompt 1 (Minimal Geometric Mark):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Single flat vector icon: a vertical shelf outline with a stylized magnifying glass or search symbol overlaid. Shelf in teal #2DD4BF, magnifying glass in purple #8B5CF6. Ultra-clean lines, 64px @ 1x. No gradients or decorative elements.

**Prompt 2 (Literary/Bookish Illustration):**
Dark background #0d0f17. A hand reaching toward a bookshelf, fingers about to grasp a spine. One book highlighted in teal #2DD4BF, others receding in purple #8B5CF6 tones. Flat vector, minimal detail, emphasis on motion & discovery. Works at 64px @ 1x.

**Prompt 3 (Wordmark-Focused):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Clean, accessible wordmark: "Shelf Finder" in a modern sans-serif. Integrate a subtle magnifying glass glyph between the two words or as a dot replacing an "i". Text in teal #2DD4BF, glyph in purple #8B5CF6. No gradients. 64px mark width @ 1x.

---

### **Spine Hunt**

**Prompt 1 (Minimal Geometric Mark):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Flat vector: a single stylized book spine viewed head-on, textured with a small arrow or target mark on its surface. Spine in teal #2DD4BF, arrow/target in purple #8B5CF6. No shading or gradients. 64px @ 1x, legible at small scale.

**Prompt 2 (Literary/Bookish Illustration):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Playful illustration: three book spines on a shelf, one glowing or highlighted in teal #2DD4BF with a small stylized "hunt" element (a compass rose or sparkle) nearby in purple #8B5CF6. Flat vector, warm line weights. 64px @ 1x, readable and charming.

**Prompt 3 (Wordmark-Focused):**
Dark background #0d0f17. Custom wordmark: "Spine Hunt" in bold, modern sans-serif. Spine icon or compass rose accent above or integrated into letterforms. Text in teal #2DD4BF, accent in purple #8B5CF6. Flat design, no gradients. 64px mark width @ 1x.

---

## Recommendation

**Stack Peach** leads for distinctiveness + memorability. **Shelf Finder** is safest for SEO & clarity. **Spine Hunt** lands between playful + literary. All three test well at 64px with proposed color scheme.
